Module 3: Multi-Factor AI Matching Algorithm

Transparent scoring engine combining deep contextual semantic embeddings with ontology skill matching and tenure evaluation.

Composite Scoring Formula

Match Score = (w_skills × Skills%) + (w_semantic × Semantic%) + (w_exp × Exp%) + (w_edu × Edu%)
1. Technical Skills (45%): Compares JD mandatory and preferred skills with candidate resume against the technical ontology with synonym resolution (e.g. AWS ↔ Amazon Web Services, K8s ↔ Kubernetes).
2. Semantic Similarity (35%): Evaluates dense 384-dimensional contextual vectors using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Sentence Transformers. Understands nuanced project experience beyond raw keywords.
3. Experience Tenure (15%): Piecewise non-linear evaluation comparing candidate total years against the minimum required years with senior bonuses and junior penalties.
4. Education Alignment (5%): Evaluates degree levels (B.Tech, M.S., Ph.D.) and STEM relevance.
